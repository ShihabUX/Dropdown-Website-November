03 / A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before
we begin.

Can we combine services?

Yes. Combine services around your needs — for example, Performance Marketing Studio with Content & Creative Studio. We'll define the combined scope and quotation together.

Is advertising spend included?

No. You fund advertising spend separately. We manage campaigns within your approved budget and agreed platforms.

Who owns the website or software?

Ownership and usage rights are established in the agreement. We recommend client-owned domains, accounts and infrastructure, with authorized technical management by Dropdown Digital.

What about subscriptions and maintenance?

Domain, hosting, subscriptions and paid third-party tools are separate unless quoted. Ongoing support and maintenance can be arranged under a separate agreement.

How long does a project take?

Timeline depends on scope and timely inputs. The Launch & Growth Suite typically includes 14–21 working days of website development, followed by 30 calendar days of ad management after launch and tracking verification. Other timelines are confirmed in the proposal.

How do you determine the final price?

Requirements, complexity, integrations and delivery scope determine the quotation. Payment schedules, revisions, rights and support obligations are documented before work starts.
